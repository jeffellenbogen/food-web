Field Research Notes

Food Web 101

Energy Flow: Arrows represent energy moving from food to eater. The arrow points from the prey to the predator.

Trophic Levels:

  • Producers: Organisms that get energy from the Sun.
  • Primary Consumers: Herbivores that eat producers.
  • Secondary Consumers: Predators that eat herbivores.
  • Apex Predators: The top predators in the ecosystem.

Tip: Every link you draw should point UPWARDS through the levels as energy moves from the ground to the sky.
