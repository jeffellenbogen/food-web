Food Web 101

Energy Flow: Arrows represent energy moving from food to eater. The arrow points from the prey to the predator.

Trophic Levels:

  • Producers: Plants that get energy from the Sun.
  • Primary Consumers: Herbivores that eat producers.
  • Secondary Consumers: Predators that eat herbivores.
  • Tertiary Consumers: Apex predators at the top of the food chain.
